Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This document is a general template provided with the software. It is not legal advice. If you operate Bappherh Builder commercially or in a regulated setting, have a qualified lawyer review and adapt it.

1. Summary

Bappherh Builder is designed to work locally in your browser. We do not run a server that receives your projects, and the app does not include advertising or analytics trackers.

2. Information we handle

Because the app runs on your device, the information below stays on your device unless you choose to share it.

  • Projects and assets — pages, styles, code, uploaded images and project names you create. Stored in your browser’s IndexedDB or localStorage.
  • Preferences — theme, autosave, grid and font choices, and the last project opened. Stored in localStorage.
  • Files you pick — images or project files you select are read by your browser and kept inside your project; they are not uploaded anywhere by the app.

3. Information we do not collect

  • No account, email address or password is required.
  • The app does not send your projects, images or code to any server.
  • No advertising identifiers, fingerprinting or third-party analytics are included.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

The app does not set tracking cookies. It uses browser storage (IndexedDB and localStorage) only to save your work and preferences.

5. Third parties and the websites you build

If you host the app yourself, your hosting provider may log standard request data (such as IP address) under its own policy. If you add embeds (for example YouTube or Vimeo videos) or external image URLs to a page, visitors’ browsers will contact those services, and their privacy policies apply. If you build a site that collects personal data, you are responsible for your own privacy notice and legal compliance.

6. Your choices and rights

You are in control of your data: export a project at any time, delete projects from the dashboard, or clear this site’s data in your browser settings to remove everything. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (access, deletion, portability). Since we do not hold your data on a server, these actions are completed directly in your browser.

7. Security

Previews run in a sandboxed frame, and user-supplied HTML is sanitised before display in the editor. No method of storage is perfectly secure, and anyone with access to your device and browser profile can access your saved projects. Do not store secrets such as passwords or API keys in a project.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change (for example, if cloud sync is added), this policy will be updated before the change takes effect, and the “Last updated” date will change.

10. Contact

For questions about this document, contact TheDev001 through the channel where you received Bappherh Builder (for example the website, repository or message where you found it).
